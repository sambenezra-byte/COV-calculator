CMAP Qualification COV Calculator

Instructions:

This tool calculates coefficient of variation (COV) values from CMAP amplitudes obtained during a CMAP qualification exercise.

  1. Enter the Trial 1 and Trial 2 CMAP amplitudes for each muscle and side.
  2. Click "Calculate COV".
  3. Review the calculated COV values.
  4. Copy the resulting COV values into the Teckro qualification survey.
  5. No information entered into this tool is stored or transmitted.
